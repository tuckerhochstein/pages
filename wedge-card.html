Bunkers
  • Standard greenside56, grip down 1"+ to match dug-in feet. Face slightly open — this is where you want the bounce. Enter an inch or two behind, accelerate through.
  • Firm or wet sand56, squarer face, shallower entry. Less bounce needed when the sand won't give.
  • Long bunker shot, 40+52, down 1", square face, normal-length swing.
  • Fairway bunkerGrip down ½", ball slightly back, quiet legs, one more club. Clean contact beats distance.
Lies & slopes
  • UphillSlope adds loft — take one more club, match shoulders to the slope.
  • DownhillSlope delofts and the ball runs — one less club, ball back, chase it down the slope.
  • Ball above feetGrip down 1–2". Expect a draw, aim right.
  • Ball below feetDo not grip down. Stay long, more knee flex, hold your posture. Expect a fade and lower flight.
  • Bare, burnt-out lieLower loft, ball an inch back, 60% weight forward. Leading edge sits closer to the turf.
  • Soft or wet turfBall a touch forward. This is where 12° of bounce earns its keep.
Two failure modes
  • DecelerationIf the short swings come up consistently short, it's not the club. The swing is slowing down before the ball.
  • The flipGripping down makes the head feel light, which tempts a throw. Shorter and quieter at the same speed — not flickier. Handle moving toward the target through impact.
